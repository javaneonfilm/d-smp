How a round runs

From the sky to the last squad

  1. 01

    Board the ghast

    Team up after the purge, then the whole lobby climbs onto one giant happy ghast flying over the island.

  2. 02

    Drop and glide

    Sneak to jump whenever you like. Your elytra carries you down and swaps back to armor the moment you land.

  3. 03

    Loot up

    Bronze, silver and gold chests everywhere. A fresh wave with better odds drops inside the zone at minute 15.

  4. 04

    Beat the zone

    The hex wall closes in. At minute 20 the last small circle starts roaming, and by minute 25 it's gone.

Protection books

Every book and every kill adds a level of Protection to your armor, all the way to IV. Kills also repair your armor with what was left of theirs.

Squads of up to four

Invite your friends to make a team, see their health in the corner of your screen, and talk with /tc.

Fair by design

One totem and one enchanted golden apple per player, the island can't be torn apart, and only what you place can be broken.
