The ride

How a round runs

The short version, in four stops.

  1. Stop01

    Queue up

    Join the server and wait in the lobby. When the round starts, you get dropped in.

  2. Stop02

    Gear up

    Loot the map, build your kit, and get your cart ready before things heat up.

  3. Stop03

    Ride & fight

    Take the fight to everyone else. Every cart on the map is a threat.

  4. Final stop04

    Last cart standing

    Outlast the lobby to win. Wins and kills climb the leaderboards.

The rulebook

Rules

Break the spirit of these and it's still punishable. When in doubt, ask staff.

Cart Battle Royale

  2. No bug or exploit abuseIncludes dupes, cart clipping, rail or cart duplication and border bypasses. Report bugs to staff.
  3. No server disruptionNo crashing or lagging the server, including cart or entity spam.
  4. No real-money or cross-server tradingExternal gambling and boost or invite rewards aren't allowed either.
  5. No teaming in solo gamesDon't split loot, team up or throw fights for another player.
  6. No stream snipingDon't use a streamer's live feed to find or ambush them.
  7. Use common senseIf it isn't listed but clearly breaks the spirit of the rules, it's still punishable.

Chat & Community
  1. Follow Discord's ToSMinimum age is 13. Serious violations may be reported to Discord.
  2. No spamNo repeated messages, walls of text, mass reactions or voice channel spam.
  3. No advertisingDon't promote other servers, socials or services without staff approval.
  4. No hate speech or discriminationBased on race, nationality, religion, gender or identity.
  5. No doxxingNever share someone's private info without their consent.
  6. No threatsDDoS, swatting or doxxing threats, even as a joke, mean a permanent ban.
  7. Keep it appropriateNo political or religious arguments, graphic content or NSFW.
  8. Don't impersonate staffDon't claim authority you don't have.
  9. No punishment evasionUsing an alt to dodge a ban or mute gets every linked account banned.
  10. No unnecessary pingingPing players and staff only when there's a real reason.

Punishments range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity.
