Zone wall shader, copied unchanged from the Mace.VIP texture pack (maceviptxt.rar, "lodestone" border).
build_pack.py copies everything in this folder except this file into the pack.

How it is driven (plugin side, com.cartroyale.zone.ZoneWall):
- An item display holding an item with item_model lodestone:border.
- The model is one flat 1x1 quad; the shader turns it into a cylinder wall.
- Quad centre = circle centre, quad side length (display scale x/z) = radius.
- The wall runs 350 blocks up from the quad (HEIGHT in config.glsl) and fades out ~50 above the camera.
