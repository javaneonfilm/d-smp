# Brand

## Name and voice
- **Name:** CartZone (was CartMC, then briefly CartRoyale, on 2026-09-30). On the site it is written CARTZONE.VIP with "CART" in white, "ZONE" in TNT red and ".VIP" small and grey. The game mode is still called **Cart Royale**, a minecart PvP battle royale.
- **Tagline:** "Last Cart Standing."
- **Voice:** short, confident, a bit playful ("Board the cart", "Climb in"). Never corporate. No pay-to-win language: cosmetics and access only.
- **Metaphors:** rails, stops, tickets, departure boards, TNT, fuses. They are used on the site already. Keep them.

## Logo
`reference/logo.png`: a TNT minecart rendered in Minecraft style (636×599). Used as favicon, header mark, hero and store art. On the site it's shown with smooth scaling (not pixelated). Keep clear space around it.

## Type (website)
- Headings: **Barlow Condensed** 800–900, uppercase, tight leading
- Body: **Barlow** 400–600
- Data, labels, IP: **JetBrains Mono**
- In game: the default Minecraft font, with a custom glyph only for the minecart icon in the resource pack

## Motifs
- Hazard stripe (powder yellow and near-black, 45°)
- Rail with sleepers
- Ticket with notches and a dashed perforation
- Amber departure board
- Ember sparks and a pixel-square explosion

## Rules of use
- **Don't copy mace.vip** or any other server's artwork, text or layout. The example images in the plan are inspiration only.
- Keep the disclaimer: "Not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang."
- Use original textures, sounds and effect names. Don't use Mojang art beyond what the Minecraft brand guidelines allow.
- Accessibility: keep text contrast readable, and respect reduced motion on the site.
