# [Server Name] | Chat & Community Rules

These apply on Discord, in-game chat, and voice channels.

1. **Follow Discord's ToS and Guidelines.** This includes the minimum age of 13. Serious violations may be reported to Discord.
2. **No spam.** No repeated messages, walls of text, excessive line breaks, mass reactions, or voice channel spam.
3. **No advertising.** Don't promote other servers, socials, or services without staff approval.
4. **No hate speech or discrimination.** Racism, sexism, and discrimination based on race, nationality, religion, gender, or identity are not tolerated.
5. **No doxxing or sharing private info.** This covers real names, addresses, phone numbers, IPs, and socials without the person's consent.
6. **No threats.** Threats of DDoS, swatting, doxxing, or serious harm mean a permanent ban, even if said as a joke.
7. **Keep it appropriate.** No political or religious arguments, graphic content, or NSFW material.
8. **Don't impersonate staff** or claim authority you don't have.
9. **No punishment evasion.** Using an alt to dodge a ban or mute means a permanent ban on all linked accounts.
10. **Don't ping without a reason.** Ping players or staff only when needed.

*Punishments range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity.*

# [Server Name] | Cart Battle Royale Rules

These apply to in-game behaviour.

1. **No cheating or unfair mods.** Hacked clients, kill aura, auto-clickers, macros, speed/fly, reach, ESP, radar, and freecam are banned. So is any mod that gives an unfair advantage.
   - **Allowed:** performance and HUD mods, zoom mods, Xaero's Minimap (Fairplay), Totem Pop Counter, HurtCam, Replay Mod, FreeLook, No Chat Reports, Ping Equalizer, BetterHitreg
   - **Disallowed:** any hacked client, macros, auto-clickers, Mouse Tweaks / Item Scroller, InvMove, Freecam (including Tweakeroo), Accurate Block Placement, FightCam, and any mod that automates cart placement, rail placement, or cart boosting
2. **No bug or exploit abuse.** This includes dupes and glitches. Report them to staff. For a cart server, that also covers:
   - Cart clipping, wall or block glitches, and rail or cart duplication
   - Cart-boosting tricks that bypass the intended speed or mechanics
   - Using any unintended mechanic to avoid the border or the arena bounds
3. **No server disruption.** Don't crash or lag the server through packet exploits, entity or cart spam, crash books, or similar. Spawning or stacking huge numbers of carts to cause lag counts.
4. **No real-money or cross-server trading.** Don't trade money, gift cards, or items from other servers for in-game advantages. External gambling and boost or invite rewards aren't allowed.
5. **No teaming or collusion (if solo mode).** *[Delete if you allow teams.]* Don't team up, split loot, or throw fights to help another player win. Players in a solo match can't work together.
6. **No stream sniping.** Don't use a streamer's live feed to find or ambush them.
7. **Use common sense.** If it isn't listed but clearly breaks the spirit of the rules, it's still punishable. If in doubt, ask staff.

*Punishments range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity.*

## Suggested additions once the plugin mechanics are final

These aren't published yet. Review them, then add them to the website's Rules section and to Discord.

**Game rules**
1. **Don't disconnect to dodge a death or a purge.** Leaving mid-match counts as an elimination.
2. **Squad limits are strict.** No teaming beyond your squad size, and no sharing loot with other squads.
3. **Only one account per person in a game.** Alt accounts in the same match are banned.
4. **Hosts run their own game, not the server.** A hoster who bought a game must follow every rule above, and staff can end a game that breaks them.
5. **Report bugs, don't farm them.** Anything that gives coins, loot or wins in an unintended way is an exploit (see the exploit rule above).

**Store rules**
6. **No chargebacks.** A chargeback removes the purchase and results in a permanent ban.
7. **Enter your exact Minecraft username** when buying. Purchases sent to a mistyped name can't be moved.
8. **Ranks and tickets are for the buyer's account only.** Selling or trading them is not allowed.
