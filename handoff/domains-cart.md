# cart.<tld> availability (checked 2026-09-30)

Method: RDAP (the official registration-data service) for every TLD that has one (1,204 TLDs),
plus WHOIS for popular country TLDs without RDAP. "Not registered" means the registry has no
registration for it. It does **not** mean it can be bought at the normal price: `cart` is a common
word, so many registries hold it back as a reserved or premium name (cart.io, for example, is
"reserved by the Registry"). A registrar's search (Porkbun, Namecheap, Cloudflare) shows the real price.

## Could not be checked

The registry behind many popular TLDs (.live, .world, .games, .group, .team, .zone, .life, .gold,
.today, .guru, .ninja, .rocks, .plus, .social, .studio ...) rate-limited or refused the lookup,
and .co, .us, .tv, .pro, .vip (already known taken), .es and .at did not answer. Check those at a
registrar.
